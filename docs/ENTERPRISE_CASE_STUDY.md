# Enterprise Case Study — Northstar Cloud Systems Vendor Review

## Scenario

A procurement and risk team must decide whether Northstar Cloud Systems may handle internal business data. The team supplies security, privacy, procurement, and resilience policies plus a vendor profile.

The challenge did not prescribe this vendor or use case. It was selected as a practical demonstration vehicle for the published enterprise agent-orchestration capability stack.

## Orchestrated delivery

The coordinator decomposes the mission. Knowledge retrieval returns cited internal evidence; vendor-intelligence tools return identity and risk signals; security, compliance, finance, and operations agents analyze separate dimensions; the independent reviewer assigns the risk posture; a draft report is attacked by a red-team agent; and the final report is released only after citation and quality gates pass.

## Demonstrated controls

- hostile instructions are quarantined and excluded from ordinary retrieval;
- viewers cannot execute restricted tools;
- high-risk recommendations pause for a risk manager;
- transient agent failure resumes from a checkpoint;
- a weak draft is rejected and repaired;
- budget limits terminate execution;
- pause, resume, and emergency stop persist;
- every decision remains in a tamper-evident audit chain.

## Measured deterministic demonstration result

The packaged self-test completed eleven agent tasks, persisted eleven traces and thirty-four metrics, verified thirty-six standard-mission audit records, and produced a final report with eight source citations.

These are technical validation metrics from the supplied demonstration—not claims of external customer savings, revenue, production deployment, or independently audited business outcomes.
