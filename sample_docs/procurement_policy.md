# Enterprise Vendor Procurement Standard

Procurement must verify the supplier's legal registration, ownership information, sanctions screening, insurance, financial stability, and capacity to perform the proposed service. Any sanctions match requires escalation to Legal and Compliance before the engagement can continue.

Material vendors must provide evidence of financial stability or a recent financial statement. Procurement should consider years in business, going-concern concerns, adverse media, concentration risk, and business continuity preparedness.

Approval decisions must cite the evidence used. Missing evidence is an open control gap and must not be treated as a positive finding. Conditional approval may be used only when remediation items, owners, and due dates are documented.

All external-system calls must use approved connectors. The vendor review record must preserve the mission objective, tool calls, agent findings, source citations, and final recommendation.
