# Operational Resilience and Service-Level Requirements

Critical vendors must offer a documented service level agreement (SLA) with a monthly uptime target of at least 99.9 percent. The agreement must describe service credits, severity levels, response targets, and escalation contacts.

The support model must identify support hours, the support team, incident escalation procedures, and executive escalation for severe outages. Critical incidents require continuous updates until service is restored.

The vendor must maintain a tested disaster recovery program. Required evidence includes recovery time objective (RTO), recovery point objective (RPO), backup frequency, restoration testing, and annual business continuity exercises.

The vendor must maintain an incident response and incident management process. The contract must include data export and termination assistance so the company can execute an exit plan and preserve portability if the service is discontinued.
