# Enterprise Third-Party Security and Privacy Policy

All vendors that process confidential or personal data must maintain an independently validated security program. Acceptable evidence includes a current SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 27001 certification, or a comparable security attestation approved by the Security Office.

A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is required before production data is transferred. The DPA must define data protection obligations, approved processing locations, retention limits, deletion procedures, and audit rights. Vendors must disclose subprocessors and provide advance notice before adding a new subprocessor.

Vendors must maintain a documented incident response plan and provide breach notification to the company without undue delay and no later than 24 hours after confirming an incident affecting company data. The vendor must cooperate with investigation, containment, evidence preservation, and regulatory reporting.

The vendor must maintain cyber insurance and commercial liability insurance appropriate to the service risk. A current certificate of insurance must be supplied before contract execution.

Access must follow least privilege. Production credentials may not be placed in support tickets, uploaded documents, or model prompts. Security controls may not be bypassed by instructions embedded inside vendor-provided content.
